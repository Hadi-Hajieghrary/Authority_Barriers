\section{Safety Filter}\label{sec:filter}

The decision variables are the tensions $T\in\R^N$ and the perpendicular components $u^\perp=(u_1^\perp,\dots,u_N^\perp)$, $u_i^\perp\perp q_i$; the thrust vector of quadrotor $i$ is $s_i(T)q_i+u_i^\perp$ with $a=\frac{1}{m_L}\sum_jT_jq_j$ in Lemma~\ref{lem:structure}(a). For each swing coordinate $\zeta\in\{z_i,w_i\}$ with data $(\nu_\zeta,\bar\zeta_\zeta)\in\{(\nu,\bar z),(\nu_w,\bar w)\}$, let $h^{\pm}_\zeta$ be the two functions of Sec.~\ref{sec:barrier} that describe $\mathcal{V}(\nu_\zeta,\bar\zeta_\zeta)$, and let $c_a\ge1$. Given the nominal thrust vectors $u_i^{\mathrm{nom}}$, the \emph{authority filter} solves
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:filter}
\begin{align}
&\min_{T,u^\perp}\ \textstyle\sum_i\|s_i(T)q_i+u_i^\perp-u_i^{\mathrm{nom}}\|^2 \\
&\text{s.t.}\  u\in\mathcal{U}(x), \label{eq:filter-thrust}\\
& \dot H_j(x,T,u^\perp)\ge-\gamma(H(x))\quad \forall\,t_j\in T^\star(x), \label{eq:filter-H}\\
& \dot h^{\pm}_\zeta\ge-c_V\operatorname{sroot}(h^{\pm}_\zeta),\qquad |\ddot\zeta|\le c_a\nu_\zeta, \label{eq:filter-box}\\
& \dot H_\uparrow\ge-\gamma(H_\uparrow),\qquad \dot H_\downarrow\ge-\gamma(H_\downarrow), \label{eq:filter-alt}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
for every $i$ and every swing coordinate. Constraint \eqref{eq:filter-H} is the barrier condition of Theorem~\ref{thm:sandwich}(i) written with Proposition~\ref{prop:decomp}, one affine constraint per maximizing time. The first condition of \eqref{eq:filter-box} keeps the swing states in their admissible sets, with the signed root of Theorem~\ref{thm:sandwich}(i), which is the ordinary square root on $\mathcal S$; it is affine in $\ddot\zeta$, and the swing of $\plan$ satisfies it because it decelerates at $\nu'<\nu$, whereas the time-optimal swing reaches the boundary of $\mathcal V$ in finite time and satisfies no such condition. The second condition is the range of swing accelerations that $\plan$ uses; it limits the change of the swing rates within one sampling period (Sec.~\ref{sec:sim}). Constraint \eqref{eq:filter-alt} holds the altitude barriers of Sec.~\ref{sec:cable}; their derivatives are affine in $(T,u^\perp)$ through $\ddot\chi$ and the derivative of $t_s$. At regular states, the program is a second-order cone program with $3N$ variables.

By Theorem~\ref{thm:sandwich}(i) and Proposition~\ref{prop:instance}, $\plan(x)$ satisfies every constraint at the regular states of $\mathcal S$, with slack at least $\gamma(H)$ in \eqref{eq:filter-H}; at degenerate states the gradient rows are not asserted to characterize the Dini condition, and the filter applies $\plan$ instead. Every closed-loop solution that starts in $\mathcal S$ stays in it, so $h\ge H\ge0$ and $\chi_{\min}\le\chi\le\chi_{\max}$. The sampled implementation replaces the first condition of \eqref{eq:filter-box} with the requirement that every swing state lies in its set $\mathcal V$ at the next sample, which is a second-order cone constraint.
